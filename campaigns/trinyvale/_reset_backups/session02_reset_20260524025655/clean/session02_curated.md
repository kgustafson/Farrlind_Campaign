# Curated Session Packet

The party began the session by reviewing the events of Session 01, including the acquisition of rewards and resources, the metaphysical shift, and the encounter with dire wolves. They traveled from the Night Lotus Inn and Spa in Icewind Dale to Barovia. During this journey, they received a note and a basket containing wine, pastries, and a letter from Strahd Von Zorovich.

Upon arrival, the party navigated a trail shrouded in thick fog, leading them to the Overseer's Manor. They discovered three human corpses in the woods near a new construction site, which they investigated, finding items they possessed. The party then faced three rising zombies, which resulted in combat.

During the confrontation, Jens Lyndelle used his high perception check to notice details on the corpses, and Nyack of the Ran'afor of the Ran'afor rolled the highest investigation check while examining them. The party used key cards to open a gate, utilizing Nyack of the Ran'afor of the Ran'afor's alpha wolf brain to arrange the key cards in the shape of a 'Z' to solve the puzzle.

The party then encountered three zombies in the woods off the trail. Jens Lyndelle pulled items from the corpses, including two short swords and a longbow, while another zombie clutched an envelope. Nyack of the Ran'afor of the Ran'afor attempted to take the longbow from a zombie, resulting in a contested strength check, and used Hunter's Mark on a zombie.

Onyx Lumiere Lumiere used the spell Misty Step to teleport to Jens Lyndelle. The party then found an envelope and read a message from the Burgomaster of Barovia detailing a curse and a request to encircle the land with symbols of good, referencing Marina Kuljana and a vampire.

The party then established a plan to shut down the hotel and franchise the proprietary burger recipe, deciding to murder the daughter of a vampire who worked at the hotel. They sought to bring the Burger Master in to learn his secrets and eventually eliminate him.

The party traveled down the road, encountering a Svalich road roll. Onyx Lumiere Lumiere and Nyack of the Ran'afor of the Ran'afor experienced a metaphysical shift related to their connection to their phones/weapons. They attempted to manifest a familiar using their journal and magic.

The party traveled through a fog, emerging into a village setting, and arrived at Barovia, finding an abandoned guard post. They planned to find the Burger Master and attempted to find a vegan restaurant in the resort town.

The party entered the resort town, finding a tavern named "Blood on the Vine." They confronted the manager, Strahd, regarding a warning posted on the gate. Strahd revealed that the Burger Master was the father of the owners and the manager of the establishment.

The party then traveled towards a mansion, passing shops like Bildrath's Mercantile and Madam Ava's Fortunes and Mortion. As they approached the mansion, the sun rose red and angry, stripping away the fog to reveal a massive stone pillar and a castle atop it.

The party confronted Strahd and his minions, who had repeatedly harassed them, leaving marks on the mansion. A confrontation ensued involving Ismark, Marina, and an imp, resulting in violence.
