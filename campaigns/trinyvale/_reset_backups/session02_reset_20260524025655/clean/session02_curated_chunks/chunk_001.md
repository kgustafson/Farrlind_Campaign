# Chunk Canon Extract

## Events

*   The party reviewed the events of Session 01, including the acquisition of rewards and resources, the metaphysical shift, and the encounter with dire wolves.
*   The party traveled from the Night Lotus Inn and Spa in Icewind Dale to Barovia.
*   The party received a note and basket from Strahd Von Zorovich.
*   The party navigated a trail shrouded in thick fog, leading them to the Overseer's Manor.
*   The party encountered three dire wolves waiting to ambush them at a large gate.
*   The party used key cards to open the gate, using Nyack of the Ran'afor's alpha wolf brain to solve the gate puzzle by arranging the key cards in the shape of a 'Z'.
*   The party received a basket containing pastries, a tote bag, a bottle of wine, and a letter from Strahd Von Zorovich.
*   The party followed the trail, encountering a new building under construction.
*   The party discovered three human corpses in the woods near the new building.
*   The party performed investigation checks on the corpses, finding items they possessed.
*   The party faced three rising zombies, which resulted in combat.
