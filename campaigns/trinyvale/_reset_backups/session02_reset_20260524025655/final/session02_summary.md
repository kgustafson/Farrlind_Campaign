## Canon Events

- The Triplets travel down the muddy Svalich Road after being dragged from the Night Lotus into Barovia. Their power has been reduced, their original gear remains missing, and they keep interpreting Barovia as bad resort management. [Svalich Road] (travel)
- Jens notices the smell of death and metal glinting off the trail; the party finds three dead commoners with muddy clothing, claw marks, crows, paw prints, and several days of decay. [Svalich Road] (discovery)
- Nyack finds a jar of pickled ghoul tongues. Nyack eats one and gains Bless for 10 minutes; Onyx eats one, suffers necrotic damage and Bane, and decides she is now vegan. [Svalich Road] (discovery)
- Fast Zombies in the Woods: the three corpses rise as unusually fast zombies. Instead of defeating them, the Triplets steal the rusty short swords, bloodstained longbow, and envelope, then flee into the mist without killing the zombies. [Svalich Road] (combat)
- The stolen envelope contains a desperate letter from Kolyan Indirovich, Burgomaster of Barovia. It says Marina Kulyana has been bitten by a vampire, that the vampire has terrorized Barovia for over 400 years, and asks outsiders to seal Barovia away with holy power so the evil cannot escape. [Svalich Road] (discovery)
- Because of the title Burgomaster, the Triplets misread the letter as a burger-magnate problem involving a proprietary recipe, Yelp reviews, and a threatened mom-and-pop burger business. [Svalich Road] (social)
- Onyx summons a new imp familiar named Bluetooth, also nicknamed Jens Z. Bluetooth is rude, Gen Z-coded, iPad-using, and pacifier-spitting; Onyx becomes attached to him in a BoyMom way. [Svalich Road] (acquisition)
- During a road encounter, Nyack trips while protecting the pickled tongue jar and Jens discovers a lantern with a black candle that burns with green flame and never runs out. [Svalich Road] (discovery)
- The forest gives way to the Village of Barovia, a bleak settlement of boarded houses, fearful watchers, sobbing, prayers, and hysterical laughter. A sign names Kolyan Indirovich as current Burgomaster. [Village of Barovia] (discovery)
- The Triplets enter Blood on the Vine while looking for vegan food and all-inclusive resort dining. Inside they see a barkeep, a lone patron, three boisterous women by the fire, and a blonde man in fine leathers. [Blood on the Vine] (social)
- Keychain disguises himself as a robotic dog wearing a wolf pelt; Onyx disguises herself as the dead messenger Dolphin; Nyack disguises himself as the zombie he necked with; Jens remains undisguised and is briefly pacified with Onyx's iPad and AirPod setup. [Blood on the Vine] (social)
- The blonde man recognizes Onyx's Dolphin disguise and asks whether he posted Kolyan's warning at the gate. He reveals Kolyan is his father, making him likely Ismark, and explains that Strahd and his minions have repeatedly attacked. [Blood on the Vine] (social)
- Ismark leads the party toward Marina, but realizes the warning letter was not posted and that the Triplets are lying badly under suspicious disguises. He slams the door before Marina can open it, draws a knife, and demands to know who they really are. [Burgomaster's House / Marina's Doorway] (social)
- The session ends on a cliffhanger when Onyx orders Bluetooth to attack; the imp spits out his pacifier and launches forward, putting the party on the verge of fighting the people they were probably supposed to help. [Burgomaster's House / Marina's Doorway] (combat)
