# Curated Session Packet

The party began their journey by traveling from the Night Lotus Inn and Spa in Icewind Dale into the gothic domain of Barovia. During this travel, they encountered dire wolves and an Alpha, resulting in a combat encounter. Upon arrival, the party found themselves in Barovia, a realm where they sought Strahd von Zarovich von Zarovich von Zarovich.

While traversing the trail, Nyack of the Ran'afor of the Ran'afor underwent a profound metaphysical shift, transforming into a gloomstalker ranger with an alpha attitude and new abilities. The party also found a basket containing wine, pastries, a tote bag, and a letter from Strahd von Zarovich von Zarovich. They investigated the area around the new construction, discovering three human corpses that possessed weapons, and the corpses subsequently rose to attack them.

The party then engaged in combat with three zombies in the woods, which were noted to possess a strange firmness and internal fire, suggesting they were more capable than normal undead. Jens Lyndelle successfully retrieved weapons from one of the zombies, and Nyack of the Ran'afor used abilities like *Hunter's Mark* and *Zephyr's Strike* to manage the combat and escape back onto the trail.

The party then decided to leave the resort area and seek out a restaurant in the town. They used a disguise, with Keychain transforming into a steel defender robotic dog, and the gloom stalker used the *disguise self* spell to transform into a zombie to navigate the area. They found a tavern named Blood on the Vine, where the bartender, Dolphin, recognized one of the party members.

The party discussed their overarching goal: eliminating the Burger Master and his daughter, who they suspected was a vampire, and seeking to make amends with Strahd von Zarovich von Zarovich. They learned that Strahd von Zarovich was paranoid about spies and that the resort town was financially unstable due to lost guests.

The party then negotiated access to Strahd von Zarovich von Zarovich's mansion, which was located behind a rusting iron fence. As they approached the mansion, the environment shifted dramatically, revealing a massive stone pillar and a castle atop it. During this interaction, a deception check failed, leading to an immediate attack by the imp familiar, Bluetooth.
