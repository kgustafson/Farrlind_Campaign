# Chunk Canon Extract

## Events

*   The party travels from the Night Lotus Inn and Spa in Icewind Dale into Barovia.
*   The party encounters dire wolves and an Alpha, resulting in a combat encounter.
*   Nyack of the Ran'afor experiences a metaphysical shift, being reborn as a gloomstalker ranger with a black fauxhawk and an alpha attitude.
*   The party uses key cards to open a gate, which Nyack of the Ran'afor uses his alpha wolf brain to solve by arranging the cards in the shape of a 'Z'.
*   The party receives a basket containing pastries, a tote bag, a bottle of wine, and a letter from Strahd von Zarovich von Zarovich.
*   The party investigates the area around the new construction, noting foul smells and finding three human corpses.
*   The party uses perception checks to investigate the corpses and finds that they possess weapons (a longbow, rusty short swords, and an envelope).
*   The three corpses rise and lunge towards the party.
