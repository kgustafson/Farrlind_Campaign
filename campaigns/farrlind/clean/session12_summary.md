Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-10-05
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Namal 6
Title: Battle of Sam

The session begins with the party member reaching out to touch Sam, only to discover his hand has become skeletal with decaying flesh falling off. The party member puts on a ring that provides protection and pushes Sam away. Corvinas responds to the growing fight at the rear of the caravan and prays for sacred flame.

Combat begins when five rangers appear and attack the party. Bridgit, Richard, Gildas, Mikani, Roon, and the party member engage in combat with the enemy rangers and Sam. Corvinas is struck by an arrow but is healed by Mikani's prayers.

Sam casts a spell dealing psychic damage to Corvinas and the party member. He then attempts to harm himself with a black dagger but fails. The party member and Corvinas continue to attack Sam, while Gildas repeats his ray attack, and Roon attacks the enemy rangers. Mikani uses her god's power to heal Corvinas and strikes an enemy ranger.

As the battle progresses, more enemy rangers are defeated or flee. One of the frogs summoned by Sam attacks Corvinas, knocking him down. The party member then tells a joke backed with power, causing Sam to laugh uncontrollably. Corvinas seizes the opportunity to strike a killing blow and sever Sam's arm.

Corvinas also throws a javelin at one of the frogs, striking it. Bridgit and Richard hit another frog with arrows. Roon attacks and defeats the last enemy ranger.

The party then turns their attention to the two remaining frogs. Gildas uses his Scorching Ray to attack one of the frogs, while Mikani attempts to shatter the other frog into pieces. Roon becomes a killing machine and attacks both frogs until they are defeated.

After the combat, the party searches Sam's body and finds the dagger used by him. Gildas examines it and determines that it is necrotic in nature, possibly from the abyssal plane. Corvinas mentions the history of the dig site and the abyssal nature of this discovery, suggesting a possible connection.
