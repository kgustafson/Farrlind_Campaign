Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-03-30

In-Game Date(s): 1832 AS Apollal 13, 1832 AS Apollal 14

Diary Title: The Village of Thataways

The party continues their search for the centaurs by the lake and use Gildas' familiar owl to help locate a large bushy tree. They encounter a centaur who takes them to the village of Thataways, where they meet a variety of fey creatures, including satyrs, dryads, and an earth elemental.

The party splits up, with Makani heading to the temple of Eerol in the worship sector of the village, where she meets with zealous centaurs and receives guidance from Namalea through running water. Meanwhile, Faban and Brigit explore the entertainment sector of the village, engaging in music and bartering.

Gildas and Corvinas visit the barter sector and meet a busker who provides information about the witches, including the witch sage who protects the village with spells. They also learn about the five sages, three druids, and one wizard who keep the village safe.

The party reunites and decides to visit the central tree to figure out how to contact the sages and obtain Urgan's Axe. Gildas approaches the two guards outside the tree and mentions that they are messengers of the queen, allowing them entry into the tree.

Inside the tree, they meet an Arakocra wizard who warns them not to touch the books and provides information about the witch sage and the coven of witches nearby. The party learns more about infernal items and how to handle them.

The sages summon the party and agree to allow them to use Urgan's Axe in exchange for taking care of the Allegiance of Stars threat. The party agrees and receives instructions on how to care for the axe.

Makani asks about Jennifer and the celestial isles, and the sages share their collective wisdom. They also provide information about other wells of magic across the world and offer to allow them to talk with their well of magic when they return with the axe.

As the party prepares to leave, a satyr approaches Faban and gifts him a fiddle imbued with magical runes.
