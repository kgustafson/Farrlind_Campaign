Title: The Journey North

The session begins with Corvinas studying a book about the archeological dig site they are traveling towards. He discovers that the location played a significant role in ancient times during the transition from the era of dragons to the era of gods. The party spends several days traveling without incident, with Teddy being described as a "geek."

On Namal 1, Roon notices strange shadows moving on their own and shares his concern with the party. Gildas uses his arcane knowledge to detect magic around the campsite, sensing necrotic divination from the Abyssal plane. He sees four lights fly away, and the magic disappears.

Mikani talks to the plants and senses fey and undead presence coming from within the caravan. She also detects celestial and fiendish influences within the party. Corvinas speaks with the rangers accompanying the caravan, noticing that they are equipped with beautifully crafted longbows and wearing light armor.

The session also introduces Sam, a mysterious figure hanging around in a rickety old carriage at the back of the caravan. He warns the party about the dangers of removing protection from the archeological site, stating that it could lead to ancient curses and the return of arcane magic.

On Namal 6, the party reaches the dig site, where they find makeshift ladders and digging tools. Corvinas tries to learn more about Sam's ring through his codex of songs and recalls a poem describing the ring as providing protection to its wearer.
