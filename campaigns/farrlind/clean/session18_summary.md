Title: The Falling and the Forge

Session Summary:

The session began with Lightdelver, a dwarf, falling from a high window in the black tower. The party used Dimension Door to teleport away from the falling wizard, but not before Gildas was struck by debris and suffered wounds.

As they appeared beneath the falling wizard, Brigit prepared an owlbear skin for use as a makeshift parachute. Mikani improvised and invoked Nameloa to slow Lightdelver's fall. Corvinas advanced cautiously, while Levitate anchored to the debris field to suspend Lightdelver in mid-descent.

After Lightdelver's safe landing, the party asked him about the Well and learned that it was located in the forge. They then visited the quartermaster, who denied them entry initially but eventually allowed them through after persuasion failed and urgency succeeded.

The party followed a mithril vein, which led to a pool of radiant violet like molten amethyst. The pool formed a face and introduced itself as Saiffi, a well bound to truth. Saiffi described himself as highly knowledgeable, although among the least powerful wells.

The party learned that seven elements govern the world: Earth, Air, Water, Fire, Order, Chaos, and Arcana. They also discovered that Ardor destroyed Guson intact and that Omnium on Pompadour was stronger than most.

Saiffi revealed that dragons are signs of impending doom, and Tiamat's return would be devastation. However, he emphasized that the party could not prevent the Cataclysm but might be able to delay it.

The session concluded with the party preparing for battle against Orsydon, a black dragon with metallic sheen, and his robed cultists.
