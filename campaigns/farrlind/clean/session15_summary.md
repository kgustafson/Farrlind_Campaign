Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-12-21
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Namal 11

We returned to Paramon beneath a thick veil of mist after the events on the shore. The bodies of the fallen were being gathered, and Corvinas examined the remnants of the water elemental, concluding that such beings are often guardians bound to place rather than malice.

The session's main event was the performance of **The Defense of the Watery Dunes**, a song recounting the battle as resistance rather than terror. The crowd gathered quickly, and the player earned 13 gold in tips.

Brigit found drink and vanished briefly into a local watering hole, while Gildas watched the crowd with wariness, noting the sigil of a fallen chaos elemental. Corvinas observed two figures speaking in sharp tones before one disappeared.

The player noticed a moment of disappearance, which was later revealed to be a distortion caused by Tasha's cruel gift. A black-furred tabaxi collapsed into mirth, and when his laughter subsided, he spoke of ancient dragonlands, the Cataclysm being not a consequence but a **requirement**.

The party returned to the Well, where they found raw arcane force thrumming through the chamber. Corvinas resisted this energy, calling upon Namaloa, which led to a brief psychic backlash. When it ended, Corvinas still stood, which was seen as significant.

Later, Mikani and Roon went to the Temple of Namaloa, but the player did not follow. They learned that Iron Paw, the head priest, is not Namaloan, wearing borrowed colors instead. Gildas spoke with a sapphire-eyed stranger in a tavern, mentioning this detail once and then not again.

The session concluded with the player writing about their experiences, noting the pattern of Wells awakening, guardians subverted, faiths infiltrated, and ages summoned. The future must be argued for, and the player chooses to remain silent at times.
