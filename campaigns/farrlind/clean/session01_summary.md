Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-02-16
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Apollal 10

The Battle

The party reacted to a mysterious being, yelling "Every battle has a chorus, and I shall lead it!" as they charged. Gildas cast Grease underneath the creature, causing it to slip in the slick fluid. Roon beat on the creature, while Makani and Gildas cast spells. The party took hits from the creature, with the protagonist nearly mortally wounded. Corvinas hit hard in retaliation, assisted by Gildas' familiar owl. Bridgit attacked the creature from behind Roon's back and disappeared quickly. As the creature regenerated itself after every blow, Gildas cast a cruel laughing spell, causing it to fall again.

Key Events:
• The party discovers that the magic source emits from the center of the rubble pile.
• Gildas detects magic and finds a round red orb emitting orange light, which is later determined to be an evocation magic orb.
• The party investigates the basement room offered by the nearby building's owners and learns about the Demon Lord of Rage.
• Makani senses that the storm is caused by the demon lord of lightning.
• The party rushes to the mayor's penthouse in the Bentrios Tower, where they find it ajar and overhear the mayor being threatened by an infernal-speaking voice.
• Roon charges into the room, only to be confronted by the demon lord who disappears in a flash.

The party interrogates the mayor about his connection to the demons and their quest for Urgan's lost great axe. The mayor reveals that the axe is rumored to be infused with Urgan's rage energy and is hidden somewhere in the central plains of the continent. He also provides a map showing an unexplored forest teeming with Fey Wild magic, where the party believes their future lies.

Session End:

The session ends with the party deciding to venture into the forest in search of Urgan's lost great axe.
