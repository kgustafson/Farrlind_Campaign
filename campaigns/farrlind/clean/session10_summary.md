Title: Attack Continues and We Begin our Journey North

The session begins with the party continuing their attack on the controller. Guildas uses arcane fire to rain down on the construct and the controller, causing the controller's robes to smolder. The controller retaliates by spraying Roon with acid, which burns his face but does not incapacitate him. Mikani calls upon her deity and casts a healing spell to mend Roon's wounds.

I use my magic to create a cloud of tiny daggers around the controller, dealing it significant damage. Corvinas throws a javelin that hits the controller, killing it. The elemental controlling the orb transforms into a Dao and attempts to attack Corvinas. However, Roon parries its blow and attacks the Dao.

The Dao summons earthen power, knocking Corvinas and Roon to the ground. Mikani takes damage from the sudden noise, but Guildas manages to create a slick of grease under the Dao's feet, allowing it to maintain its footing. Mikani then uses her electrical magic to strike the Dao with lightning bolts.

I move my cloud of daggers onto the Dao's space and use healing magic to repair Roon's acid burns. Brigit appears out of nowhere and fires an arrow at the Dao, missing but causing it to become disoriented. Corvinas attempts to attack the Dao but misses. Roon takes advantage of his flanking position to strike the Dao again.

The Dao attacks those around it, but its lack of visibility due to my cloud of daggers causes it to miss all three attacks. Roon reposts one of the attacks and connects against the Dao. The Dao attempts to conjure earthen power once more, but Brigit and I jump away in time. Mikani takes damage from the noise but manages to avoid being hit.

Guildas uses his fire magic to strike the Dao with heated plasma rays. Mikani directs lightning at the Dao's head, causing it to explode. The party then collects themselves and gathers the remnants of the orb.

The next day, we meet with the caravan leader Richard and set off on our journey north. We discuss archeology with some members of the caravan and learn about a dig site in the Northern mountains. Teddy is excited to be part of the group.
