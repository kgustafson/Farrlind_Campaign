Title: The Sea Held at Bay

The party met Samuel and proceeded to the underground city. They discovered the well named Nibane, which was inactive for more than 10 years. After resolving the immediate threat of flooding and the appearance of Niebain/Nebain, the party successfully used spellcasting abilities (*Control Water*, *Sanctuary*) to manage the flooding and combat.

The party confronted a monstrous Merfolk-like creature emerging from a hole and engaged in combat against a Marrow using web traps and physical attacks. They managed to defeat the Leviathan, a gargantuan water creature acting as a damage sponge. The party also discussed complex combat mechanics, including advantage, sneak attacks, and damage rolls.

The party was advised to seek the Monastery of the Open Hand to gain trust and reverse damage caused by Salazar. They established diplomatic roles (Mikani, Faban, Corvinus) and gained access to the city, seeking an audience with the Queen to access the hidden magical well.
