Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-04-26
In-Game Date(s): 1832 AS Apollal 16, 1832 AS Apollal 17
Title: The Day in Bentrios

The party begins the session by securing transportation to return to Thisaway and the Fey forest to return the axe. As they approach the Fey forest, they see a large lizard attacking the edge of the forest.

In battle mode, the party attacks the lizard, with Brigit hitting it with an arrow, Gildas casting a spell causing grease to form under the creature, Roon shooting it with a crossbow, and Makani casting a spell that causes Roon to glow with a magical shield. Corvinas engages the lizard but is eventually grabbed by its massive maw and lifted off the ground. The party continues to attack the lizard, with Gildas shooting a firebolt into it, Brigit hitting it again, and Makani casting another spell. The lizard is eventually killed.

After defeating the lizard, the party sets up a hasty camp and heals. They then return to Thisaway and go to the tree, where they are allowed in without question from the guards. They find Cleras reading as normal in the library and return the axe. They also ask to talk to the well of magic below the tree and learn about the other wells.

The party descends into the well, where they meet a face that forms over an opening. The face responds to Gildas' questions, providing information on the location of one of the wells and stating that it has five siblings. The well also mentions that all wells are bound to tell the truth at all times, and that Khorag is connected to nature.

The party then heads to the tavern, where Roon talks to the military and learns about their strategy for protecting Thisaway. Soon, bells ring out in the street outside, indicating an attack on the city. Roon goes with the guards to defend the city.

Meanwhile, the rest of the party emerges from the well and heads to the tavern, where they see storms and hear laughter coming from the clouds. Gildas' owl senses a creature in the clouds, as storms rage and winds swirl. The party reunites outside the tree and goes inside, where Cleris asks them to defend the tree.

A human appears, looking somewhat beaten up, with a robe of an archmage and a staff of lightning. He calls himself Ardema of the seven seals and is a warlock. Mikani casts spells at him to no effect, and the party prepares for battle.
