Physical Session Date: 2025-02-02
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Apollal 10
Title: The Party forms

The session begins with background information on the continent of Farrlind and its main city, Bentrios. The diary source provides a detailed description of the setting, including the city's technology and warforged entities.

On Apollal 10, the party forms at Alexander's Inn in Bentrios City. A halfling named Bridgit enters the tavern, ignoring the entertainment provided by the narrator. Roon, a fighter, soon follows, attempting to recruit the narrator into the watch of the city. The paladin Corvinas and blue dragonborn Makani also arrive, adding to the gathering crowd.

The narrator notices that Gildas, an elf wizard, has arrived, searching for something. Later, it is revealed that he seeks an artifact. The warforged waiter at the tavern glitches and fails, prompting Gildas and Corvinas to investigate similar incidents throughout the land. The narrator presents a well-made card sorter as an example of proper craftsmanship, which promptly fails and burns up a set of cards.

As the party prepares to leave, Roon receives a message from the watch, seeking his assistance at headquarters due to issues throughout the city. He enlists the help of the narrator, Corvinas, Bridgit, Makani, and Gildas. The group rushes into the streets of Bentrios to assist in any way they can, with a large rock being thrown at them.
