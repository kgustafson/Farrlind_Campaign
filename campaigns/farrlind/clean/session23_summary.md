Session Summary:

The party traveled from Catur to The Gale, discussing the mechanics of spellcasting and potential enchantments along the way. They also acquired valuable assets and travel enhancements from the Queen.

Upon arrival in The Gale, the party used a ritual to commune with nature, gaining information about the location of the Monastery of the Open Hand and the well. However, the specific location of these sites within The Gale remains uncertain.

The party gained knowledge about the dangerous nature of the region, including the elemental beings (Ice Methods, Air Elementals, Pyramidons, Myrmidons) and patrols ("father trolls"). They also discussed the full history and implications of the song from Norhundra regarding white dragons, but only partial information was revealed.

Combat occurred when a white dragon emerged from the snow, and the party successfully fought it.
