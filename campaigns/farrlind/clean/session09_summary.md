Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-08-10
In-Game Date(s): 1832 AS Apollal 19, 1832 AS Apollal 20, 1832 AS Apollal 21
Title: Bellemaine

The party began the session by researching the wells and magic at the library. Gildas found a book about the Elemental Cataclysm, which spoke of controlling all the wells of magic to bring about destruction. Mikani searched for information on the dark forces and found a book on primordial chaos that she couldn't read, but Gildas helped her understand it by casting Comprehend Languages.

The party then visited the stables to find the quartermaster's office and met Aracokin, who offered to accompany them to survey the site in return for protection. They also met Thompson, a naturalist quartermaster who provided information about Jen and the druid commune. Mikani asked about learning at the commune with the nature book.

The next day, Corvinas found a book on devil hierarchy, while Roon hung out with Brigit and Mikani. Half-giant Rebar offered to arm wrestle Roon, but Roon lost 5 gp. The night ended with the party sleeping.

On the final day of the session, the city was attacked by a construct, which was being controlled by an individual holding up an orb similar to one Gildas had captured earlier. The party scrambled to respond to the attack. Gildas shot fireballs at the controller and the elemental, while Mikani called down lightning bolts from the sky. Corvinas prayed and laid hands on Roon to heal him, and Roon raged with his sword ignited by divine power.
