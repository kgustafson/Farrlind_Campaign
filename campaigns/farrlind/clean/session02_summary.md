Title: Visit to the Temple of Knowledge

The session begins with the party visiting the Temple of Knowledge in search of answers about the orb and the Demon Lord of Rage. They meet Father Joseph, who explains that the construct attacking them was likely controlled by another being, similar to a marionette artist. He also recommends conveying the orb with a padded carrying case.

The party then sets out for the Fey Wilds, traveling through the city and noticing the mechanical things have stopped working, bringing back old ways like sundials and oil lamps. They come across a blacksmith trying to get his failed warforged to carry on and ask about the Great Axe of Urgan. He doesn't know where it is but shares that it was crafted by Dwarves a millenia ago.

As they travel through the day, they encounter an old man who warns them not to make deals with the fey and mentions a pack of gnolls about a half-day from the woods. They also meet Oak, a dryad, who agrees to help them in exchange for their vow of silence on where the axe is located.

Oak shares that she has made a deal with another party, cannot tell them more about the Demon Lord of Rage, and suggests seeking assistance from a Satyr or Centaur for safe passage through the woods. She also mentions that a hag or gnoll could be of more help.

The party heads west to find the lake where they can meet the centaur. During their watch, some members see visions of people from the town. The next day, they encounter a blue-skinned fey creature who asks them to lead the gnolls to the Satyr camp in exchange for information about the Great Axe of Urgan. They refuse, and an encounter begins.
