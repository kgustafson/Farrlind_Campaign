Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-08-02
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Apollal 18
Title: New Journey

The party woke up still sore from their previous night's encounter. Gildas, Brigit, Mikani, and Corvinas sought out information about Jennifer in the tree's library. They found a red book that described the history of extra-planar creatures and the nine hells. Gildas discovered a map to the commune in the north.

While searching for information, Brigit tried to steal the red book but was caught by Claris. He agreed to copy the map for the party. Mikani didn't find anything, and Corvinas learned about his past encounter with Rage, the demon from the 8th circle of hell.

Meanwhile, Roon and I headed to the tavern where we were staying. We heard rumors about strange happenings in the woods south of here, including a dragon's roar and sightings of Jennifer. The captain of the guard agreed to escort us to the approximate location.

The party reunited and set out with the centaur captain to the forest area in the south. Gildas detected a patch of druidic energy not recognized by him. He dug down into the dirt, hit metal, and uncovered a hatch.

We followed a glowing mushroom path and found a circle of mushrooms. Gildas landed his owl in the circle, but his communication was cut off. We entered the circle and saw a large clearing filled with fungi. A giant firbolg named Zakana petted Gildas' owl. He told us about the spore sanctuary and helped us leave.

We found ourselves at the exterior of the sanctuary, which was nowhere near where we were in the fey woods. Zakana gave us 10 mushrooms each to heal 1 hp. We asked about the nearest city and he mentioned Bellemaine, an elven city starting with the letter B.

The party traveled to find Bellemaine, an upscale and peaceful city. I performed with the local elven band, including [The Song of the Whispering Wood](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1K0hV9IGGx6cZbSIYCNWAWc5iKgZGpnGTENs-kxDB2RY/edit). Food and drink cost us 1gp for the night.
