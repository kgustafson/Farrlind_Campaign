Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-11-16
In-Game Date(s): 1832 AS Namal 11
Title: Another Day

We began the session with a peaceful morning, enjoying vegan food and discussing celestial matters with Jennifer. She shared her concerns about the Cataclysm and the resurfacing of old problems. The group spoke again about the Wells, including Catur and Paramon.

The party decided to explore Paramon, where they found an old man tracing sigils near a ring of standing stones. He offered to arrange a portal for 50 gold per soul. After stepping into the circle, the group was transported to the sea, where Mikani sensed kuo-toa, humans, and other presences.

The party discovered an arcane well beneath the guild hall and met Ordor, who warned them about impending danger. However, before they could react, Biha-Bibir and a water elemental appeared, attacking Roon and Corvinas. The group responded with divine wrath from Mikani and Corvinas' thunderous smite.

Combat ensued, with the party facing off against the sea's fury. Whelm and whirlpool controlled the weather, but Mikani's trident managed to split the elemental in two. When the water stilled, Ordor was silent, leaving behind an uncertain future for the Wells.
