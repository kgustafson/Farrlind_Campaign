Title: In Defense of the Tree and the Well of Magic

The session begins with a battle against Ardema, who uses eldritch energy bolts to attack Faban and Makani. Gildas deflects the attacks with his shield, while I conjure many blades of Melkior to counterattack. Gildas levitates Ardema, allowing Brigit to shoot him with her short bow. Mikani calls upon their deity to smite Ardema down. The party also uses other abilities and spells, including Corvinas' longsword imbued with fiery magic and Claris' magic missile.

However, the tide of battle turns when Ardema casts a spell called Shatter, killing both Makani and Faban instantly. The rest of the party is injured in the process. Gildas attempts to cast Tasha's giggle but has no effect on Ardema. Brigit and Corvinas continue to attack, with Claris joining in as well. Roon attacks again, but misses. Ardema, feeling trapped and bloodied, casts lightning at Brigit before plane-shifting out of the fray.

After the battle, the party attends to their dead comrades and heals their wounded. During a discussion about what happened earlier, I mention coming up against four individuals, which sparks interest from Claris. I then sing a ballad about these people, causing Mikani and Brigit to roll their eyes. The party eventually makes it back to the inn, where they rest and I busk the night away.
