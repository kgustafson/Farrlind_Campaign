Title: The Battle of the Fey Witch

The session takes place in-game on Apollal 13, where the party engages in combat with a fey witch. The witch summons two goblins to fight for her, and Roon and Corvinas quickly take down one of them. The party's fighter, however, is not as successful, as the witch counters with magic missiles and brings the fighter to an inch of death.

The party tries to find the witch's location after she disappears, but Corvinas manages to bring the fighter back to health using his healing abilities. The witch suddenly reappears near Gildas, prompting a furious attack from the party. Roon is struck with a killing blow that brings him to death's door, but the party is able to bring him back with more healing.

Corvinas ultimately delivers the final blow to the witch, beheading her as she turns to dust. The party discovers a satchel left behind by the witch, which appears to contain nothing at first. However, after poking the bag with a stick and throwing in a copper piece, a few souls fly out along with an ancient-looking tome.

The fighter collects their copper piece, while Gildas examines the tome, which appears to be necromantic and ancient.
