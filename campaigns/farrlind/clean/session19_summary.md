Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2026-04-12

Session takes place during the Balrog/Saiffi arc. The party located the Balrog town center/tower area and learned cultists were trying to summon a dragon. The party decided to help Saiffi and put him in a wineskin.

Cultists summoned the dragon Orsydon. Combat involved Orsydon, cultists, party positioning around cover/pillars, and party members using attacks and spells. Orsydon used nightmare breath, dealing psychic damage and causing frightened effects.

Faban and Lightdelver both used Cloud of Daggers; Faban called it "Cuisenart." The dragon was defeated. Lightdelver lost concentration and became unconscious during the fight.

After the combat, the party returned toward the city/tavern and continued planning around the wells, the Wand of Wells, and the cataclysm.
