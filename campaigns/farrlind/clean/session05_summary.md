Session Summary:

Physical Session Date: 2025-03-30
In-Game Date: 1832 AS Apollal 14, 1832 AS Apollal 15
Title: The Return to Bentrios

The party reaches the edge of the forest and thanks their centaur escorts from the Messengers of the Queen. They then travel towards Bentrios, with Gildas sending his owl familiar, Yew, to scout ahead. Corvinas notices movement in the brush and feels like they are being followed. Brigit stealthily checks out what's going on and sees a fey creature intent on stealing their purses. She attacks the creature, which goes catatonic in fear, and Makani figures out that it is a leprechaun.

The party searches for the leprechaun and Gildas' Yew spots it nearby, enjoying the gold piece given to it. The leprechaun says it was trying to steal the axe, but disappears after being persuaded by Makani. The party camps out and has a quiet night with no encounters.

On the second day, they travel to Bentrios, where tower renovations are underway and magic shops are affected by the changes in technology. They decide to visit the temple of Siath to find Father Joseph. Gildas explains that they have recovered the axe, which may be connected to Salazar's plans. They encourage Father Joseph to take over from the mayor of Bentrios, who is embroiled in controversy and corruption.

The party heads to the mayor's office, where they demand that he return the axe when he is done using it. The mayor agrees, but says he will do his best. Outside, lightning strikes and Salazar appears, examining the axe and reciting an incantation. He mentions being under an infernal contract and that he must "fix things" for the Baron in return for certain artifacts, including the axe.

Salazar fulfills his part of the bargain and shunts the city back to a point in time when it was at its most prosperous. The city reverts in time, the tower collapses, and warforged begin disappearing. Salazar disappears with a wink, leaving behind Father Joseph, who mentions that there are well springs of magic that have been opened.

The party learns that they need to learn how to close the wells from the wells of magic themselves. They also discover that the island of Haunidal has one of these wells and that it is unknown. The cataclysm is known to form from natural storms created under unnatural circumstances, which sounds similar to the destruction of the outer isles.

The party heads to a nearby tavern for the night, where they gather information about the Alliance of Stars, a small adventure party based in Bentrios.
