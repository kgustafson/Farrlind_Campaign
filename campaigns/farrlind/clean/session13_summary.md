Title: Aftermath and parting of ways

The session begins with the group discussing the obsidian dagger they discovered, which has a hooked blade used for tearing flesh. They also review their journey and prepare to head to the druid retreat.

During their hike up the mountain, they encounter heavy rain and wind, seeking shelter in a shallow cave. Inside, they meet an old man named Cole who offers them a deck of cards with pure magic. Roon draws "The Star," which signifies growth and evolution, while Gildas pulls "The Rogue," which harbrings betrayal.

Cole disappears, leaving the group to rest and continue their journey. The next day, they experience no major issues, but Mikani senses a solidity with nature due to the conspicuous absence of influence by the planes.

As they approach an encampment, Corvinas gets trapped in vines that suddenly seize him. An unseen archer shoots arrows at them, one containing a parchment reading, "Those who seek, harm, or burn, shall face the wrath of many here." Roon is injured by another arrow.

An elvish archer named Faban appears from the brush, revealing herself to be Jennifer, the object of their quest. She seems uneasy when they discuss the Axe of Urgan being shown to demons and informs them that she no longer possesses the wand. The wand was stolen recently due to issues with magic and unreliable protection spells.

Jennifer relates information about the wells: Monastery of the Open Hand (human), Thataway (Fey Elves), Hanedal (Tiefling, rumored to be able to stay open), Beaches to the East (Pearamon Elves), Sunken City of Catur (Triton), and Dwarven City of Balrog (Dwarves). She explains that the wells are "kind" and don't seek retribution, but it's crucial to stop the cataclysm and seek all the wells.

Jennifer leads them to her coven, where they discuss their journey and the situation. She warns them not to put full trust in the gods, as they are flawed like mortals. Many people in her coven can help them on their journey.
