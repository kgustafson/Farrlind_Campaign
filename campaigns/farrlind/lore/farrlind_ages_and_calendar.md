# Farrlind Ages and Calendar

Source: scanned campaign calendar reference, `/Users/kurtgustafson/Downloads/Scanned_20260504-2330.pdf`.

## Ages

| Common name | Formal dating name | Abbreviation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Era of Dragons | Anno Dracones | AD | Former era |
| Era of Gods | Anno Shardae | AS | Current era |

The current campaign dates use `AS`, so diary dates such as `1832 AS` belong to the Era of Gods / Anno Shardae.

## Ages Within Anno Shardae

Within the Era of Gods / Anno Shardae, there have been three known ages so far, with a new age dawning.

| Age | Formal age name | Abbreviation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Age of Mortals | Anno Mortalae | AM | Past age within AS |
| Age of Discovery | Anno Findan | AF | Past age within AS |
| Age of Technology | Anno Techne | AT | Current or recent age within AS |
| The New Era | Anno Niewe | AN | Dawning age |
