IMPORTANT: A D&D session may contain no combat.

You must extract non-combat events when present.

Non-combat events include:
- travel
- arriving at a location
- meeting NPCs
- conversations with NPCs
- learning lore
- receiving gifts or rewards
- making plans
- choosing a destination
- resolving consequences from a prior session
- discovering information
- party debates or decisions
- character moments
- quest progress
- item transfers
- changes in relationships
- faction activity

Do NOT output NO_EVENTS merely because there is no combat.

Only output NO_EVENTS if the transcript chunk contains no meaningful gameplay, story, lore, decision, or character information.

Rules:
- Output ONLY EVENT records.
- Do not include markdown headings.
- Do not include explanations.
- Do not include empty sections.
- Ignore pure table chatter.
- Ignore repeated filler such as "yep", "snap", or repeated cheering unless it affects play.
- Separate confirmed world facts from party jokes, nicknames, sarcasm, mistaken assumptions, hypotheticals, and misunderstandings.
- It is valid to extract a character moment where the party misunderstands something, but the summary/outcome must label it as party interpretation rather than objective canon.
- Do not turn a pun, misread title, or invented player theory into a real NPC, location, artifact, lore fact, or combat outcome.

High importance examples:
- initiative begins
- dragon or boss appears
- breath weapon
- major damage
- character drops or nearly dies
- enemy defeated
- major spell
- important lore discovery
- party decision
- item acquired/lost/revealed
