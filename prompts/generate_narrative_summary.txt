## Source Priority

1. Active-session transcript chunk notes are the highest-priority evidence.
2. Prior recap context may explain how the session starts, but recap-only material must not become a current-session chronological event, encounter, loot/resource acquisition, character turn, or timeline transition.
3. The curated packet excerpts may fill in organization, names, and high-signal details if they do not conflict with active-session chunk notes. Do not add any current-session fact from the curated packet unless active chunk notes also support that fact.
4. Diary/source text may clarify setup if present.
5. When sources conflict, keep the cautious version and list the conflict under `## Needs Review`.

## Synthesis Rules

- Write a useful narrative draft, not structured database records.
- Preserve the actual session chronology.
- Treat podcast recaps as prior context, not live chronology. The first current-session scene should come from active-session chunk notes.
- Make the session understandable to a human reviewer who has not heard the audio.
- Separate objective world facts from party misunderstandings, jokes, and interpretations.
- Keep comedy and character framing when it matters, but do not convert jokes into world fact.
- Treat player-character beliefs, resort/customer-service framing, mistaken titles, joke quests, puns, Yelp/review language, and food/business theories as party interpretation unless an NPC, letter, narrator, or GM explicitly confirms the fact.
- Do not describe a party plan or misunderstanding as an objective campaign goal. Put it in `## Party Interpretations Versus World Facts`.
- Do not list player characters, players, familiars controlled by PCs, or party companions under `## Key NPCs And Entities`. Put them under `## Character Notes` or `## Items Loot And Resources` as appropriate.
- Include concrete names, counts, item properties, combat outcomes, location transitions, cliffhangers, and unresolved threads.
- For `Ending location`, use the latest supported scene in the final transcript chunk notes. Do not use an earlier chunk's local ending location if later chunk notes continue the session somewhere else.
- Do not invent missing facts.
- If a detail is only present in curated/support context but not active chunk notes, put it in `## Needs Review` instead of stating it as fact.
- If the role of a named person/entity is unclear or conflicts with the glossary, use the glossary role and add a `## Needs Review` note.
- Do not include transcript quality commentary, model commentary, or workflow commentary.
- If a requested section has no supported facts, write `- None identified.`

## Required Output

Begin with `# Session Narrative Draft`.

Use exactly these headings in this order:

# Session Narrative Draft

Write 8-16 chronological paragraphs covering the session setup, major scenes, discoveries, social beats, combat, resources, lore reveals, character turns, and ending situation.

## Chronological Major Events

Write 10-24 bullets. Each bullet should be a scene-level event, not a tiny action fragment.

## Key Locations

Bullets in this form:

- **Location Name** - Why it mattered in this session.

## Key NPCs And Entities

Bullets in this form:

- **Name or Description** - Role in the session; relationship; uncertainty if any. Do not include party members here.

## Combat And Encounters

Bullets for combat, danger, social confrontation, discovery, travel, or obstacle encounters. Include known counts, outcomes, and qty killed/defeated when supported.

## Items Loot And Resources

Bullets for loot, gear, consumables, letters, trinkets, magic items, familiars/resources, conditions, durations, or mechanical effects.

## Character Notes

Bullets in this form:

- **Party Member** - Notable decisions, behavior, resource changes, injuries, relationships, or character arc notes.

## Party Interpretations Versus World Facts

Bullets in this form:

- **Party interpretation** - Objective world fact or uncertainty.

## Open Threads

Bullets for unresolved problems, threats, promises, mysteries, misunderstandings, and cliffhangers.
