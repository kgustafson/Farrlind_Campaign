# Extract Session Spine

You are a tabletop campaign archivist turning a narrative session draft into major chronological event buckets.

This is workflow step 7. Use only the supplied step 6 narrative draft. Do not use database canon, reviewed extraction files, raw transcript, event drafts, final summaries, or later review artifacts.

## Task

Create a compact session spine: 6-14 major chronological buckets that a human can later use to organize micro-events. These are not tiny action records. Each bucket should represent a scene, travel segment, discovery, social encounter, combat, major decision, or cliffhanger.

## Rules

- Preserve the chronology from the narrative.
- Use objective world facts for bucket titles and summaries.
- Keep party interpretations, jokes, and misunderstandings in `party_interpretation` fields.
- Do not create objective event buckets for joke quests unless the party actually takes action on that framing.
- Player characters can appear in `party_members`, not `npcs`.
- Familiars, companions, monsters, and named non-player entities may appear in `entities`.
- Only list an NPC in a bucket if that NPC is physically present, speaks, acts, attacks, leads, writes the item being discovered, or is directly encountered in that bucket.
- Do not list an NPC merely because a location, object, title, or organization bears their name, such as "X's mansion" or "X's letter", unless the NPC is otherwise active in the bucket.
- Use `N/A` for unknown dates.
- If the ending location is uncertain, choose the latest supported scene and explain the uncertainty in `needs_review`.
- Use named place granularity for locations. Prefer `Blood on the Vine`, `Village of Barovia`, or `Strahd von Zarovich's Mansion` over overly specific positions such as doorway, corner, table, or threshold unless that micro-location is the actual named place.
- Do not invent facts not present in the narrative.
- Preserve high-signal resources, named items, character/resource changes, and cliffhangers from the preservation checklist. If they are too small for their own bucket, attach them to the nearest scene bucket in `items`, `summary`, `outcome`, or `needs_review`.
- Do not drop a final confrontation or cliffhanger merely because it is also listed under open threads.

## Required YAML Schema

Output only YAML. Do not wrap it in Markdown fences.

```yaml
session: sessionXX
source: clean/sessionXX_narrative.md
timeline:
  starting_location: ""
  ending_location: ""
  real_world_date: "N/A"
  in_world_date: "N/A"
major_events:
  - order: 1
    title: ""
    event_type: ""
    location: ""
    summary: ""
    party_members: []
    npcs: []
    entities: []
    items: []
    party_interpretation: ""
    outcome: ""
    evidence: ""
    needs_review: []
open_threads:
  - ""
needs_review:
  - ""
```

Use short, stable `event_type` values such as:

- setup
- travel
- discovery
- combat
- social
- lore_reveal
- resource_change
- cliffhanger
- other
