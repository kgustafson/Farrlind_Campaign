# Session 19 Summary — The Battle of Balrog Square

Session 19 opened in Balrog, the underground dwarven city, with the party catching everyone up on the immediate crisis. In the prior session, the party had saved a falling dwarf wizard after he was thrown from a high tower. Faban used Dimension Door to get Gildas beneath him, and Gildas saved the wizard from dying. The wizard then helped lead the party to the local well of magic, Scythe / Safi, who was weak enough that the party carried him away in a wineskin inside Gildas’s Bag of Holding for protection. When the party returned from the mines, a group of reckless cultists had successfully summoned a black, cave-dragon-like creature in Balrog’s town square.

The battlefield was the town square, not the tower itself. The dragon had come down into the square, with cultists positioned around it. Collapsed and standing pillars provided cover, with intact pillars offering strong protection and collapsed ones offering partial cover. The party began combat roughly ninety feet from the dragon, with several cultists between them and the creature.

Brigit opened the fight aggressively. She attempted to hide behind a pillar, failed to hide from the dragon but remained unseen by the cultists, and then landed a sneak attack on one of the cultists. The shot dealt enough damage to kill the cultist outright, removing one of the summoners from the field immediately.

The remaining cultists retaliated with dark energy attacks, described as Eldritch Blasts or similar necrotic/force magic, and also conjured Spiritual Weapon attacks against Makani and Brigit. The blasts struck some of the party, though the summoned weapons failed to connect. The fight quickly established that these cultists were not harmless fools, even if they had been mocked as “emo kids” who did not understand what they were doing.

The dragon then took to the air or moved with terrifying speed and unleashed Nightmare Breath. This was not ordinary fire or acid; it caused psychic anguish and fear. Makani and Corvinus took the full force of the breath, suffering heavy psychic damage and becoming frightened, while most of the others took reduced damage. Brigit avoided the blast entirely. The breath weapon established the dragon as something stranger than a standard black dragon: a cave-dragon-like horror whose attack struck at the mind.

Gildas attempted to restrain or control the dragon with Web, but the dragon resisted. Faban then gave Bardic Inspiration to Roon by singing from the song of Urgan / Ergen Wormbane, trying to bolster the fighter for the coming clash. Faban also attempted to use Enemies Abound to convince the dragon that the cultists were its enemies, but the dragon’s resistances and saving throw prevented the spell from working. Faban’s turn still mattered because Roon received inspiration for the melee that followed.

Roon closed with the dragon and began carving into it with his greatsword. The dragon attempted a pounce or bite-like counterattack, but Roon blocked it with his shield and used Riposte, spending a superiority die and Faban’s Bardic Inspiration to land another hit. In one early exchange, Roon dealt a major amount of damage and became the party’s front-line anchor against the dragon.

The party continued pressing the fight. Makani used her dragonborn breath weapon, and the dragon critically failed its saving throw, taking a strong burst of damage. Corvinus used healing and defensive magic to keep himself in the fight after the Nightmare Breath. Brigit continued using sneak attack opportunities as Roon held the dragon in melee. Roon also clarified the properties of his greatsword of life stealing: on a natural 20, it deals extra necrotic damage and grants him temporary hit points, provided the target is not a construct or undead.

Faban and the rescued dwarf wizard then became a surprisingly effective magical pair. Faban cast Cloud of Daggers at 4th level, cutting into the dragon with a whirling mass of blades, and then continued inspiring Roon. The dwarf wizard, seeing the tactic, cast his own Cloud of Daggers, adding a second field of magical blades. Together, the overlapping dagger clouds tore into the dragon and helped overcome its regeneration or healing.

After sustained attacks from the party and the two overlapping clouds of daggers, the dragon finally fell. The finishing damage came as the dagger magic cut through the dragon’s healing, and the creature was defeated. As it collapsed, Roon and the dwarf wizard had to avoid the falling body. Roon succeeded well enough to take reduced damage, but the dwarf wizard — referred to in the transcript as something like Mouth-a-lug / Narthaluck — was crushed beneath the dragon and knocked down.

The fight was not fully over once the dragon died. Remaining cultists still had to be dealt with. Faban stepped up and stabbed one of the wounded cultists, killing him with an emphatic amount of damage. Faban then initially intended to give Roon more Bardic Inspiration, but reconsidered when he realized the dwarf wizard was down, instead using Healing Word to keep him alive. The revived wizard crawled free and used Lightning Bolt against the remaining cultists, taking at least one more down. Corvinus also moved to heal the wizard further.

Once the square was secure, the party gathered themselves and celebrated their victory. They went to a better part of town for dwarven hospitality, food, and ale. Brigit took one sip of dwarven ale and promptly passed out despite a strong Constitution roll, while Faban performed for the crowd, singing of the battle and the defeat of Orsydon — the dragon’s name as discussed at the table. The party’s victory made them local heroes in Balrog.

During the celebration, Scythe / Safi began speaking from the bag of holding, still contained in the wineskin. The party uncapped him, and he confirmed he was alive but very much wanted to be returned to his home. Faban, Gildas, and others escorted the well back down into the mines, where they poured him back into his lava-like resting place.

Once restored, Scythe gave the party important lore. The party told him they had already spoken with Ordor and were likely heading toward Catur, the sunken city. Scythe warned that the remaining wells were more wary of mortals than he was and might not be easily persuaded to help, even with the cataclysm underway. He said ancient things could persuade them — things whose current locations even he did not fully know — but suggested the party may already possess one. The party discussed possible candidates, including the broken orb fragments, Corvinus’s fiendish curse/holy touch, and Roon’s necromantic sword, though Scythe implied the sword was not what he meant.

The well also said the Wand of Wells was not easily stolen from the druids, implying that whoever took it was not mortal. When asked whether the thief might be immortal, fiendish, or otherwise otherworldly, the well only confirmed the important point: “not mortal.” He also described the current crisis as abnormal and against the natural order.

The session ended with the party safe in Balrog after defeating the dragon, restoring Scythe, and learning that the next wells may require persuasion through ancient celestial or fiendish objects. The party also officially leveled from level 7 to level 8.

## Key Locations

Balrog — The underground dwarven city where the session’s major battle took place.

Balrog Town Square — The main battlefield, with pillars, cultists, and the summoned dragon.

Balrog Mines / Scythe’s resting place — The hidden location where Scythe / Safi belonged and where the party returned him after the battle.

Celebration tavern / higher-class dwarven district — Where the party celebrated the victory, Faban performed, and Brigit was defeated by dwarven ale.

## Key NPCs / Entities

Scythe / Safi — The weakened well of magic in Balrog. Temporarily carried in a wineskin, later restored to his place. He warned that the remaining wells may distrust mortals and may require ancient persuasive objects.

Dwarf Wizard / “Mouth-a-lug” / Narthaluck — The dwarf wizard previously saved from falling. He fought beside the party, cast Cloud of Daggers and Lightning Bolt, was crushed by the falling dragon, and was saved/healed afterward.

Cultists — Reckless young summoners who brought forth the dragon and used dark magic against the party.

Orsydon — The black/cave-dragon-like creature summoned in Balrog Square. Defeated by the party.

## Important Lore

The remaining wells are not guaranteed allies. They have seen the worst of mortal kind and may be far less willing to help than Scythe.

Ancient items of fiendish or celestial nature may persuade the wells, and the party may already possess one.

The Wand of Wells was stolen by something not mortal, which raises the stakes beyond ordinary theft or politics.

The cataclysm is not normal and runs against the natural order.

## Rewards / Progression

The party gained the gratitude of Balrog after defeating the dragon and protecting the city.

The party officially advanced to level 8 at the end of the session.

## Session Endpoint

Session 19 ended with Scythe restored, the dragon dead, Balrog saved, and the party preparing for the next stage of the well quest — with Catur, Hanidal, and The Gale still ahead.
