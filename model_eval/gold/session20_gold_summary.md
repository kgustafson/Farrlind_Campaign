# Session 20 Summary — Gifts of Balrog and the Road Toward Catur

Session 20 opened in the aftermath of the party’s victory over the black dragon. The group recapped that they had returned the well, and the well warned that the other wells may not be helpful without persuasion. The persuasive objects may already be in the party’s possession, and they are likely ancient things of fiendish or celestial nature. This led the party to discuss possible candidates: the fragments of the broken orb, Roon’s greatsword of life stealing, Corvinus’s demonic mark, Mikani’s medallion from the Celestial Isles, and the still-mysterious object connected to Faban’s satchel/book. The well also said that the outer islands are under unnatural stress, against the natural order.

The party confirmed they had reached level 8 after defeating the dragon, and then decided their next major destination would be Catur, the sunken city, rather than the Monastery of the Open Hand. They reviewed the major unvisited places still ahead: The Gale, Catur, and Hanidal. The Gale was described as a wound in the middle of the continent, while Catur was understood to be underwater.

Before leaving Balrog, the party separated for errands, prayers, resupply, and possible magical improvements. Faban took his viol/instrument to a forge master to see if anything could be done to improve it. The forge master could not directly enhance the instrument in a normal way, but he pointed Faban toward the Magus Council, where magical enchantments and martial improvements might be possible.

Mikani and Brigit went to the temple district. The major temple in Balrog was dedicated to Hephasis, the god of the forge, with smaller temples to Christa, goddess of the earth; Mihira, goddess of justice; and Wurushka, god of war. Mikani chose to give thanks at Christa’s temple because there was no temple to Namaloa and Christa felt closest to that reverence for nature. A priest there prayed with Mikani and gifted her a Cap of Water Breathing, explaining that it would help in treacherous water. The cap could be attuned to a new command word and allowed both underwater breathing and full movement in water without treating it as difficult terrain.

Roon stocked up on survival provisions, including rope, hooks, and other practical gear. In conversation with a provisioner or supplier, he learned more about Catur. The sunken city is not welcoming to outsiders, and its people include tritons, kuo-toa, and some merfolk. The people of Catur call surface dwellers “above folk,” and they have had bad dealings with them. Rumors also circulate about treasure in Catur, including something called the Pearl of Atlantia / Antenella — spelling uncertain — though the source suggested such tales may simply be outsiders imagining sinister reasons for Catur’s territorial nature.

At the Magus Council, Faban leaned hard into the “saviors of the city” angle and asked for the “savior of the city discount.” The Council was already working on enchanted items, and the party’s role in defeating the dragon gave them credibility. Brigit’s shortbow was enchanted into a Shortbow of Warning, granting advantage on initiative and preventing the party within 30 feet from being surprised unless incapacitated.

Gildas encountered the dwarf wizard the party had previously saved, who still felt indebted to them. As repayment, the wizard gave Gildas a Staff of Defense. The staff grants +1 AC while held and has 10 charges, which can be spent to cast Mage Armor or Shield without using normal spell slots; the staff regains charges daily at dawn, but risks destruction if the last charge is spent and a d20 roll comes up 1.

Roon received a new enchanted dwarven shield, replacing his ordinary shield and raising his Armor Class significantly. The table joked that everyone initially ignored the beautiful shield because Roon was more excited about showing off a rock, but mechanically the shield became a major defensive upgrade.

Faban received the Acheron Blade, a black-bladed rapier with a dark nature. Mechanically, it is a +1 rapier, granting +1 to attack and damage rolls. While holding it, Faban can use an action to gain 1d4 + 4 temporary hit points. It also has Disheartening Strike: when Faban hits a creature, he can fill the target with dread, causing disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the end of Faban’s next turn; the effect fails if the creature is immune to being frightened. The transcript notes the initial ruling as once per dusk/day, with the later Session 21 recap clarifying the final cadence.

Corvinus received a magical flame weapon — referred to later as a flame sword or flame-tongued longsword — useful for the coming underwater mission because its magical flame can still function underwater, though underwater enemies may be resistant to fire. This detail was discussed more fully in the next-session recap, but the item itself was part of the gift set from this transition out of Balrog.

The group then traveled toward the western coast near Catur and reached a fishing commune outside the sunken city. Faban spoke with large, gray-skinned giant fishermen who were hauling in massive fish. The fishermen helped explain the local situation and gave useful information about the way toward Catur. The area around Catur is about six miles offshore, beyond the range that could be sensed from shore by a three-mile spell radius.

Makani used Commune with Nature to assess the region. The spell confirmed that the only nearby settlement within range was the fishing commune, and that Catur itself lay farther out at sea. It also revealed no immediate large threats nearby, and identified the most prevalent beast as magically altered giant fish — valuable, beefy-tasting fish being caught by the fishermen.

The party learned about water-breathing mushrooms, each good for one hour underwater. They also confirmed that Gildas had already made 20 potions of water breathing, each also lasting one hour. Since Mikani now had the cap, the others could divide the mushrooms and potions among themselves for underwater travel. The session established the resource math for the coming Catur approach and left the party preparing to head beneath the waves.

## Key Locations

Balrog — The dwarven underground city where the party rested, received thanks, visited temples and the Magus Council, and prepared for the next journey.

Temple of Christa — A quiet earth-goddess temple where Mikani prayed in gratitude and received the Cap of Water Breathing.

Magus Council — A magical crafting/enchantment group in Balrog where several party items were improved or granted.

Coast near Catur — The western coast/fishing commune used as the staging point for reaching the sunken city.

Catur — Confirmed as the next destination: a sunken city roughly six miles offshore, inhabited by tritons, kuo-toa, and merfolk, and unfriendly to outsiders.

Giant Fishermen — Provided local color and useful information near the coast outside Catur.

Provisioner / Mercenary supplier — Helped Roon stock up and provided warnings about Catur’s people and attitude toward outsiders.

The wells may not all be willing allies. They may require persuasion, and the key may be ancient objects tied to celestial or fiendish power.

The outer islands are under abnormal stress, against the natural order.

The party still has unresolved questions about the orb fragments, possible fiendish or celestial artifacts, and Faban’s hidden book/satchel.

Catur’s people distrust “above folk,” and the city is not likely to welcome outsiders easily.

## Session Endpoint

The party finished preparations in Balrog, reached the coast near Catur, gathered intelligence from fishermen and nature magic, confirmed that the sunken city lies six miles offshore, and prepared to use water-breathing resources to reach it.
